# My Favorite Movie

Title: Baby Driver (2017)

About the Movie

Baby Driver is an action crime movie about a talented young getaway driver named Baby who uses music to help him concentrate while driving. He gets involved in dangerous robberies but dreams of leaving his criminal life behind.

## Why I Like It

The car chase scenes are exciting.

The music matches the action perfectly.

The story is entertaining and unique.

The driving skills of Baby are amazing.

## My Favorite Character

Baby – I like him because he is talented, smart, and passionate about music.

## My Favorite Part

My favorite part is the exciting car chase scenes with amazing music.
